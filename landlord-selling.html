Landlord Selling With Tenancy

What changes when a rented home is sold.

Important: This guide is for general information, not legal, tax or professional advice. Rules may change, and your signed agreement and circumstances matter. Check official sources before acting. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

A sale during an existing tenancy raises practical questions about viewings, notices, rent instructions, deposits and the continuing tenancy. Keep communications and any formal ownership-change documents organised.

01

Start with the document

Check the signed tenancy agreement, inventory and any written amendments or messages that form part of the arrangement. The exact facts and wording matter.

02

Keep evidence

Keep relevant photos, receipts, payment records and messages. Good records make it much easier to establish what happened and what was agreed.

03

Resolve it clearly

Communicate the issue and proposed solution clearly. If both sides agree to change an existing arrangement, record that agreement in writing.

PRACTICAL RENTAL SCENARIO

Does selling a tenanted home end the lease?

A buyer wants vacant possession but the lease has months left.

What to do next

  1. Check the lease and sale conditions.
  2. Clarify the deposit and rent arrangements.
  3. Communicate verified changes to the tenant.

JC’s practical takeaway: A sale does not automatically cancel a valid tenancy.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

REAL RENTAL SITUATION

Does the tenant have to move out if the owner sells?

A rented condominium is listed for sale and the tenant fears the lease will immediately end.

What I would check next

  1. Check the tenancy agreement and sale arrangements before making promises.
  2. Clarify whether the buyer is purchasing subject to the existing tenancy.
  3. Confirm rent payment instructions and deposit handling on completion.
  4. Put any agreed early surrender or variation in writing.

JC's takeaway: Clear communication and a dated written record help both sides avoid preventable disagreements.

REAL-LIFE RENTAL QUESTION

Does the tenant have to move out if the landlord sells?

A buyer purchases a tenanted condo and expects vacant possession immediately.

What I would check first

Check whether the sale is subject to tenancy; review the lease and sale arrangements; confirm who receives rent and handles the deposit after completion.

JC's practical takeaway: A sale does not automatically erase a valid tenancy; the transaction documents and applicable law matter.
