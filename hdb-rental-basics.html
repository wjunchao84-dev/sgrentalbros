HDB Rental Basics

Eligibility, approval, occupancy and rental periods.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

HDB rentals have eligibility, approval and occupancy rules that are separate from the tenancy agreement. Check whether you are renting the whole flat or only a bedroom: the conditions are not the same.

Sources checked: 9 October 2026 · Singapore

01

Whole-flat rental

Only eligible Singapore Citizen flat owners may rent out the entire flat, generally after meeting the Minimum Occupation Period. Owners of Prime and Plus flats cannot rent out the whole flat, even after their 10-year MOP. HDB approval is required.

02

Bedroom rental

Owners of eligible 3-room or larger flats may rent out permitted bedrooms with HDB approval. Owners and authorised occupiers must continue to physically live in the flat with the tenants. Partitioned areas are not automatically approved bedrooms.

03

How long can the tenancy be?

HDB requires at least six months per rental application. The usual maximum is three years when all tenants are Singaporeans or Malaysians, or two years if a tenant is a non-Malaysian non-citizen. Renewals require a fresh application.

04

Occupancy and eligibility

Tenant eligibility depends on citizenship, pass type and other conditions. HDB occupancy caps vary by flat type and can change: larger flats currently have a temporary higher cap until 31 December 2028. Check HDB's live tables and obtain approval before move-in.

JC’S PRACTICAL TIP

For an HDB tenancy, ask for the approval letter and confirm that every occupant is registered. Never rely on a verbal statement that the flat is approved.
