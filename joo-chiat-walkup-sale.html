THE HOME

Space, character and a neighbourhood with soul.

Tucked along Joo Chiat Road, this fourth-storey walk-up offers a nostalgic way of living amid colourful shophouses, independent cafés and the rich heritage of the East.

Spanning approximately 1,033 sqft, the home has a practical, rectangular layout with generous living and dining spaces. Originally configured as three bedrooms, it has been reworked into two spacious bedrooms plus a dedicated walk-in wardrobe room, alongside a separate utility room.

Enjoy the neighbourhood's distinctive blend of old-school eateries, cafés and everyday conveniences, with Katong and the East Coast lifestyle close by.
