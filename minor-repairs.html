Minor Repair Clause

Who pays when something breaks?

THE SIMPLE VERSION

The minor repair clause usually sets out how smaller repair costs are allocated between landlord and tenant, subject to the wording of the tenancy agreement.

01

What should you check?

Check the repair cap, what caused the problem, whether it is repair versus replacement, and any specific exclusions.

02

Common mistake

A dollar cap does not automatically make every defect the tenant's responsibility. Cause and the signed wording matter.

03

What should you do next?

Read the exact wording in your signed tenancy agreement and keep the relevant messages, receipts, photos or documents. If both parties agree to vary something, record the agreement clearly in writing.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Rental situations can look similar but turn on one sentence in the tenancy agreement. Send me the clause or explain the situation before assuming who is responsible.
