Diplomatic Clause

When can a tenant end a lease early?

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

A diplomatic clause is a negotiated contract term that may let a tenant end a fixed-term lease early when specific qualifying circumstances occur. It is not automatically included in every Singapore tenancy.

Sources checked: 9 October 2026 · Singapore

01

What normally triggers it?

The actual clause may specify events such as an overseas job transfer or loss of employment eligibility. The qualifying event must match the wording. Do not assume resignation or any job change qualifies.

02

What conditions should you read?

Check the minimum stay, advance notice, required documents, whether rent is due during notice and any commission-reimbursement provision. There is no single set of terms that applies to all leases.

03

Example, not a standard rule

If a negotiated clause permits termination after 12 months with two months' written notice, the earliest practical exit depends on the exact wording and how the dates are calculated. This is an illustration, not a statutory entitlement.

04

What should you do?

Read the signed clause before giving notice. Notify the other party in the required form, keep evidence of delivery and agree in writing on the handover and final payments.

JC’S PRACTICAL TIP

Don't promise a tenant that a diplomatic clause applies until you've checked both the triggering event and the exact notice/minimum-stay wording.
