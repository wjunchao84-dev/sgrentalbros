Change of Landlord

Rent, notices and continuity after ownership changes.

Important: This guide is for general information, not legal, tax or professional advice. Rules may change, and your signed agreement and circumstances matter. Check official sources before acting. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

When ownership changes, tenants should receive clear, verifiable instructions about matters such as future rent payments and points of contact. Do not redirect rent based only on an informal message.

01

Start with the document

Check the signed tenancy agreement, inventory and any written amendments or messages that form part of the arrangement. The exact facts and wording matter.

02

Keep evidence

Keep relevant photos, receipts, payment records and messages. Good records make it much easier to establish what happened and what was agreed.

03

Resolve it clearly

Communicate the issue and proposed solution clearly. If both sides agree to change an existing arrangement, record that agreement in writing.

PRACTICAL RENTAL SCENARIO

Should rent go to a new bank account immediately?

A tenant receives an unverified message claiming a change of owner.

What to do next

  1. Verify ownership and payment instructions.
  2. Request written notice and deposit details.
  3. Keep the existing payment trail.

JC’s practical takeaway: Do not change bank details based on an unverified message.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Don't rely on what is “usually done” when the signed agreement says something specific. Read the clause first, then deal with the actual situation.

REAL RENTAL SITUATION

The owner changes. Where should I pay rent now?

A tenant receives a message from an unfamiliar number claiming to be the new landlord and asking for rent to be sent to another account.

What I would check next

  1. Verify ownership and the change through trusted independent channels.
  2. Request written notice identifying the new payee and effective date.
  3. Clarify deposit custody and ongoing maintenance contacts.
  4. Keep the original tenancy agreement and all verified notices.

JC's takeaway: Check the applicable rules and signed agreement, then confirm decisions in writing.

REAL-LIFE RENTAL QUESTION

Who should the tenant pay after the property is sold?

A tenant receives a message from someone claiming to be the new owner with different bank details.

What I would check first

Verify the ownership change and authorised payment instructions independently; get written notice of the new contact and deposit arrangements.

JC's practical takeaway: Never change rental payment details solely because of an unverified message.
