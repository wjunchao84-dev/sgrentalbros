Early Termination

What if someone needs to leave early?

THE SIMPLE VERSION

Ending a fixed-term tenancy before its contractual end date depends heavily on the signed agreement and whether an applicable termination clause exists.

01

What should you check?

Review diplomatic or break clauses, minimum stay, notice requirements, reimbursement provisions and any agreement reached between landlord and tenant.

02

Common mistake

Get any negotiated early surrender or settlement in writing rather than relying on a verbal understanding.

03

What should you do next?

Read the exact wording in your signed tenancy agreement and keep the relevant messages, receipts, photos or documents. If both parties agree to vary something, record the agreement clearly in writing.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Rental situations can look similar but turn on one sentence in the tenancy agreement. Send me the clause or explain the situation before assuming who is responsible.
