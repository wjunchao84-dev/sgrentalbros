Aircon Servicing

Why do servicing receipts matter?

Important: This guide is for general information, not legal, tax or professional advice. Rules may change, and your signed agreement and circumstances matter. Check official sources before acting. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

Many Singapore tenancy agreements require tenants to service the air-conditioning regularly and keep proof of servicing.

01

What should you check?

Follow the frequency stated in the agreement, use a suitable contractor and retain invoices or receipts throughout the tenancy.

02

Common mistake

Skipping contractual servicing can complicate responsibility if the system later develops a problem.

03

What should you do next?

Read the exact wording in your signed tenancy agreement and keep the relevant messages, receipts, photos or documents. If both parties agree to vary something, record the agreement clearly in writing.

REAL RENTAL SITUATION

Do tenants legally have to service aircon every three months?

A tenant services the aircon only once before moving out; the landlord points to a quarterly servicing clause.

What I would check next

  1. Read the frequency and contractor requirements in the signed agreement.
  2. Keep dated invoices throughout the lease.
  3. Report unusual noise, leaks or cooling problems early.
  4. Distinguish routine servicing from breakdowns and major repairs.

JC's takeaway: Read the signed terms, document the facts and agree practical next steps in writing. Contract terms and legal requirements are not the same thing.

REAL-LIFE RENTAL QUESTION

Must a tenant service the aircon every three months?

At handover, the landlord requests quarterly servicing receipts, but the tenant remembers only two services.

What I would check first

Check the actual servicing clause and frequency; keep invoices; report unusual cooling or leaks promptly; agree responsibility for breakdowns separately.

JC's practical takeaway: Quarterly servicing is common in some agreements, not a blanket legal requirement.
