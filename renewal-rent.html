Renewal & Rent Changes

What to discuss before extending the tenancy.

Important: This guide is for general information, not legal, tax or professional advice. Rules may change, and your signed agreement and circumstances matter. Check official sources before acting. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

A renewal is a chance to revisit rent, duration and other terms. Start discussions early enough for both sides to consider alternatives and record the final agreement properly.

01

Start with the document

Check the signed tenancy agreement, inventory and any written amendments or messages that form part of the arrangement. The exact facts and wording matter.

02

Keep evidence

Keep relevant photos, receipts, payment records and messages. Good records make it much easier to establish what happened and what was agreed.

03

Resolve it clearly

Communicate the issue and proposed solution clearly. If both sides agree to change an existing arrangement, record that agreement in writing.

PRACTICAL RENTAL SCENARIO

Can rent increase when a lease renews?

The landlord proposes a higher rent while the tenant expects the old price.

What to do next

  1. Check renewal option wording.
  2. Discuss the new term and rent early.
  3. Sign a written renewal agreement.

JC’s practical takeaway: Renewal terms depend on the lease and negotiation.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Don't rely on what is “usually done” when the signed agreement says something specific. Read the clause first, then deal with the actual situation.

REAL RENTAL SITUATION

Can the landlord increase rent halfway through my lease?

A landlord sees market rents rising and asks for a higher monthly payment before the fixed term ends.

What I would check next

  1. Check the agreed rent, lease term and any review or variation clause.
  2. Discuss renewal terms early enough to avoid a rushed decision.
  3. Confirm the new rent, dates and any changes in a written agreement.
  4. Check whether the variation or new lease has stamp-duty implications with IRAS.

JC's takeaway: Check the applicable rules and signed agreement, then confirm decisions in writing.

REAL-LIFE RENTAL QUESTION

Does a tenant have an automatic right to renew at the same rent?

A tenant wants another year, but the landlord proposes a higher rent and different terms.

What I would check first

Check for a valid renewal option and its conditions; start discussions early; record new rent, dates and clauses in writing.

JC's practical takeaway: Renewal terms are usually negotiated unless an enforceable option provides otherwise.
