Curtain Cleaning

A small clause that often becomes a handover issue.

Important: This guide is for general information, not legal, tax or professional advice. Rules may change, and your signed agreement and circumstances matter. Check official sources before acting. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

Curtain cleaning is often overlooked until move-out. Check whether the agreement requires cleaning, dry-cleaning, receipts or simply return in an acceptable condition.

01

Start with the document

Check the signed tenancy agreement, inventory and any written amendments or messages that form part of the arrangement. The exact facts and wording matter.

02

Keep evidence

Keep relevant photos, receipts, payment records and messages. Good records make it much easier to establish what happened and what was agreed.

03

Resolve it clearly

Communicate the issue and proposed solution clearly. If both sides agree to change an existing arrangement, record that agreement in writing.

PRACTICAL RENTAL SCENARIO

Must curtains be dry-cleaned?

Curtains look clean but the agreement specifies dry-cleaning.

What to do next

  1. Read the return-condition clause.
  2. Check fabric care and required receipts.
  3. Document pre-existing stains.

JC’s practical takeaway: Follow the signed agreement and avoid unsuitable cleaning.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Don't rely on what is “usually done” when the signed agreement says something specific. Read the clause first, then deal with the actual situation.

REAL RENTAL SITUATION

Do curtains always need dry-cleaning at handover?

A tenant washes curtains at home but the tenancy calls for professional dry-cleaning with receipts.

What I would check next

  1. Check whether the contract requires washing, steam-cleaning or dry-cleaning.
  2. Follow fabric care labels to avoid damage.
  3. Retain receipts where required.
  4. Photograph existing stains and agree how pre-existing issues are handled.

JC's takeaway: Read the signed terms, document the facts and agree practical next steps in writing. Contract terms and legal requirements are not the same thing.

REAL-LIFE RENTAL QUESTION

Are curtains required to be dry-cleaned before handover?

A tenant washes the curtains at home but the tenancy says professional dry-cleaning is required.

What I would check first

Read the precise clause; confirm fabric care instructions; agree exceptions or alternatives in writing; retain the cleaning receipt if required.

JC's practical takeaway: Curtain dry-cleaning is a common negotiated term, not a universal Singapore law.
