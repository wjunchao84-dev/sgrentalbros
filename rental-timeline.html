Rental Timeline: From Offer Accepted to Move-In

What happens next, who handles it, and when?

Important: This is an illustrative Singapore residential rental workflow, not a legally fixed timetable. Actual deadlines and obligations depend on the tenancy agreement, property type and applicable regulations. Read the full disclaimer →
THE SIMPLE VERSION

Once an offer is accepted, confirm the terms, complete the necessary checks, sign the tenancy agreement, arrange payments and stamp duty, prepare the home, and record the condition when keys are handed over.

Official guidance reviewed: 10 October 2026 · Singapore

Use this as a practical checklist for a typical private residential tenancy. The “When” column is a suggested sequence, not a promise that every transaction can finish in a set number of days. Some steps happen together.

StepWhenWhat happensWho usually handles it?
1. Offer acceptedDay 0Confirm rent, lease term, start date, furnishing, occupants, pets and special conditions in writing. Acceptance may still be subject to verification, documentation and agreed conditions.Landlord, tenant and their agents (if any)
2. LOI / good faith deposit, if usedAfter acceptanceRecord the Letter of Intent terms, amount paid, how it will be applied and when it is refundable or forfeitable. An LOI or good faith deposit is not compulsory for every rental.Parties and agents
3. Verify parties and eligibilityBefore entering tenancyVerify landlord authority, tenants' and occupiers' identities and valid immigration passes where applicable. Agents must complete relevant CEA immigration/Women's Charter and customer due diligence checks. For HDB, confirm eligibility and HDB requirements.Landlord and agents; tenant supplies documents
4. Draft and review the TABefore signingCheck rent due dates, deposit, maintenance, repairs, aircon, cleaning, access, termination, renewal, occupants, inventory and agreed special terms. Ask for corrections before signing.Parties and agents
5. Sign the TAAgreed signing dateAll required parties sign the final tenancy agreement. Keep a copy. Confirm the deposit and first-rent payment schedule; verify bank details using a trusted channel.Landlord and tenant
6. Pay rent / security depositAs specified in TAPay any balance of the security deposit and advance rent by the agreed deadlines. Record receipts and how any earlier good faith deposit was credited.Tenant pays; landlord confirms
7. Stamp the tenancy documentAfter signing, within IRAS deadlinesArrange e-stamping and keep the Stamp Certificate. IRAS generally allows 14 days after signing in Singapore, or 30 days after receipt in Singapore if executed overseas, without late-stamping penalty. Electronic-document rules may affect when/where execution occurs.Party liable under the TA, or representative
9. Apply for SP utilities accountAfter signing; before move-inWhere the tenant is responsible for utilities, apply to open an SP Group utilities account and select the intended start date. Arrange electricity, water and gas services as applicable, and allow time for any required activation appointment. Confirm that the previous account holder will close their account at the agreed time. Check the tenancy agreement if utilities are included in rent or managed by the landlord.Tenant usually applies; landlord / outgoing occupant coordinates closure
9. Prepare the propertyBefore handoverComplete any agreed cleaning, aircon servicing, repairs and furnishing. Check appliances, locks, keys, access cards and remotes. Keep relevant receipts.Landlord / appointed contractors
10. Arrange move-in mattersBefore move-inArrange internet if needed. For condos, check management registration, moving permits and deposits. For HDB rentals, obtain the required approval before renting out the flat or bedrooms. Confirm the utilities activation date arranged in Step 8.Tenant, landlord and management as applicable
11. Handover and inventoryAgreed handover dateWalk through the home, test important items, photograph its condition and confirm inventory, keys, access cards, remotes and meter readings. Both parties keep the handover record.Landlord, tenant and agents
12. Report move-in defectsPromptly after handoverSend dated photos and a written list of existing issues. Follow any defect-reporting or warranty period actually stated in the TA; there is no universal one-month rental warranty.Tenant reports; landlord responds under TA
13. During the tenancyThroughout leasePay rent, follow agreed maintenance obligations, keep servicing records and communicate repair issues early. Save important notices and payment records.Landlord and tenant
JC’S PRACTICAL TIP

Before handing over keys, make sure the signed TA, agreed payments, property readiness, access arrangements and inventory are all accounted for. An early key handover should be documented separately if it is before the lease commencement date.
