Handover & Inventory

Protect both sides from day one.

THE SIMPLE VERSION

A detailed inventory and condition record creates a common reference point for the landlord and tenant at move-in and move-out.

01

What should you check?

Record keys, furniture, appliances and existing defects. Take clear dated photos and report discrepancies promptly.

02

Common mistake

Do not rely only on memory. Small details at handover can become large disagreements later.

03

What should you do next?

Read the exact wording in your signed tenancy agreement and keep the relevant messages, receipts, photos or documents. If both parties agree to vary something, record the agreement clearly in writing.

JC'S PRACTICAL TIP

Rental situations can look similar but turn on one sentence in the tenancy agreement. Send me the clause or explain the situation before assuming who is responsible.
