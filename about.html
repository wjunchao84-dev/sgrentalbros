MY STORY

Experience matters. So does sincerity.

I've been in Singapore real estate since 2008, working with homeowners, landlords, tenants, buyers and sellers across different stages of their property journey.

Over the years, I've learnt that people don't just need someone to market a home or arrange a viewing. They need someone who explains the details, flags potential problems and gives practical advice—even when that means recommending a different approach.

I'm quite an easy-going person, and I believe talking about property should feel comfortable, not stressful. Whether you have a simple question or a difficult situation to work through, you can always have an open conversation with me. No pressure, no complicated talk—just honest advice and a willingness to listen.

One thing I'm especially thankful for is the good relationships I've built with tenants staying in properties I manage. I try to be approachable, responsive and fair to both landlords and tenants, even when there are repairs, disagreements or unexpected issues to sort out. To me, looking after a property also means looking after the people involved.

That's how I prefer to work: easy to talk to, sincere in my advice and committed to following through.
